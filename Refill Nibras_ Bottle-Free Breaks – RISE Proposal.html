Refill Nibras: Bottle-Free Breaks

Nibras International School, Dubai, UAE · Week 2 Project Proposal

Step 1 · Problem

During breaks, many students buy and throw away single-use plastic water bottles. In Dubai's heat, students drink often, but there are too few convenient refill points, so bottles fill the canteen-area bins every day.

Evidence we can collect

  • Daily count of plastic bottles in the canteen-area bins (2-week baseline)
  • Survey of 60 students: bottles bought per week, reasons, refill access
  • Photo map of where working water fountains/refill points are

Step 2 · SMART goal

Within 8 weeks of launch, our RISE team will reduce single-use plastic bottles in the canteen-area bins by 30% compared with our 2-week baseline, by setting up one approved student-run Refill Station and recording bottle counts and refills every week.

  • S – one station, one area, bottles only
  • M – 30% fewer bottles; weekly bin count + refill tally
  • A – students count, promote and maintain; staff approve
  • R – targets the observed bottle waste
  • T – 8 weeks after approval

Step 3 · Proposed solution

  • Refill Station next to the canteen: a cooler with taps, drip tray and a sign
  • "Fill & Go" sticker tally board: one sticker per refill
  • Class challenge: reusable-bottle pledge with a weekly green-house leaderboard
  • Student Refill Monitors on a rota: clean tray, restock cups, log counts

How we measure impact

  • Baseline: 2 weeks of daily bottle counts before launch
  • During: weekly bottle count + sticker tally for 8 weeks
  • After: compare average bottles per day; repeat survey
  • Success = 30% or more reduction with results shared at assembly

Make it stronger

  • Impact: fewer bottles, cooler students, less cost for families
  • Feasibility: one station, one location, small team
  • Evidence: baseline counts before anything changes
  • Sustainability: monitor rota and handover to new RISE members
  • Team improvement: add a bin-lid photo check so counts are verified

Project filter

REAL bottles are visible in bins daily   DOABLE small, approved, student-run   MEASURABLE weekly counts vs baseline

45-second pitch

"We noticed that hundreds of plastic bottles are thrown away at break. Our evidence will be daily bin counts and a student survey. We propose a student-run Refill Station by the canteen. Our SMART goal is to cut bottles in the bins by 30% within 8 weeks."
